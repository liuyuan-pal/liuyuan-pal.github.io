Intelligent Graphics Lab

We study intelligent methods for understanding, reconstructing, and creating the 3D world. Our current research focuses on 3D reconstruction and generation, motion capture and generation, and 3D-aware video-based world models.

  • 3D reconstruction and generation
  • Motion capture and generation
  • 3D-aware video-based world models

Our projects and open-source code can be found on [IGL-HKUST GitHub].
